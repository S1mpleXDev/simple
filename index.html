Demo Texture Maker

A local pixel-art editor for the Minecraft Bedrock wolf texture (64×32). Nothing is uploaded; the UV guide is generated by the editor.

Windows
Switch between Template, 3D and Split at the top. In Split, drag the divider between the windows to resize them. Every panel can be shown or hidden.
Draw on the 3D model
In the 3D window, Paint mode draws straight onto the wolf with the selected tool (pencil, brush, eraser, fill, picker, lighten, darken, replace). Drag off the model, use Orbit, two fingers, or right mouse to rotate.
Zoom
3D: wheel, pinch, the slider or +/−. Template: wheel, pinch or +/−. Pan with two fingers, the Pan tool, middle mouse or Space + drag.
UV guide
Coloured boxes show where each part of the wolf (head, ears, muzzle, body, mane, legs, tail) lives on the texture and which face is which.
Image
Place an image, move, resize or rotate it, then Stamp to bake it into the active layer.
Export
The pack writes textures/entity/wolf/wolf.png (plus tame and angry variants) and a manifest for Bedrock.
Shortcuts
P pencil, B brush, E eraser, F fill, I picker, L line, R rect, O ellipse, M move, S select, H pan, G grid, Ctrl+Z / Y, Enter stamp, Esc cancel.
iPhone & iPad
The layout keeps clear of the notch, Dynamic Island and home bar in both orientations.
